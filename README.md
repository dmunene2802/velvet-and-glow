# Velvet & Glow — Online Beauty Shop

A storefront for hair care, nail products, makeup, body care and fashion accessories, with prices in Kenyan Shillings. Customers can browse, add to cart and check out, and orders are sent to the shop on WhatsApp.

## Editing the shop

Everything you'll usually change is at the top of `js/products.js`:

- **Shop details:** name, WhatsApp number, phone, email, location
- **Delivery fees** and the free-delivery threshold
- **Products and prices:** a price of `null` shows "Price on request"

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `css/styles.css`: design and layout
- `js/products.js`: shop settings and product catalogue
- `js/app.js`: cart, wishlist, filters, search and checkout

Hosted with GitHub Pages; no build step needed.
